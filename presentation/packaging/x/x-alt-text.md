## 2. Hero Loop (`x-loop.mp4`)
> Alt text: A screen recording of the OUTGROWN user interface showing the transition sequence. A button labeled 'COMMIT & TEST PREDICTION' is clicked. An observation card representing query duration moves inside the claimed boundary and settles. Three other cards (latency, memory, and connection saturation) appear outside the boundary, while a replication lag card appears in open space. The boundary line then dynamically contracts, reducing in size, and its label changes from 'EXPLAINS THE FULL INCIDENT' to 'EXPLAINS PART OF THE INCIDENT'. The cards themselves remain stationary.
