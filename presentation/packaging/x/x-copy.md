# X Post Copy & Campaigns

This document outlines the three direct post copy variants optimized for different X audiences.

---

## 1. Variant A: Concise Launch Post
*Best for broad product design and engineering audiences.*

> What if the evidence still supports your explanation—but no longer fits inside it?
> 
> OUTGROWN is a restrained forensic prototype that helps teams track the validity boundaries of their assumptions during critical incidents.
> 
> The evidence outgrew the explanation. 
> 
> 👇 [Watch the Demo]
> https://outgrown.vercel.app

---

## 2. Variant B: Narrative Post
*Focuses on the human cognitive bias during incident response.*

> Most engineering teams fail incident response not because their hypotheses are wrong, but because they believe they are complete.
> 
> Once a plausible cause is found, "explanation lock-in" blinds us to outside anomalies.
> 
> OUTGROWN is an reasoning instrument for recognizing when an explanation remains supported but no longer accounts for the full incident.
> 
> 🎬 Full demo:
> https://outgrown.vercel.app

---

## 3. Variant C: Technical & Product Design Post
*Focuses on UI implementation, framer-motion, layout reasoning, and responsive design.*

> Designing interfaces for reasoning, not just dashboards.
> 
> For Day 11, we built an editorial tool using React + Framer Motion. 
> 
> Highlight: The signature boundary contraction. The explanation frame shrinks while supported observations remain anchored and outside observations sit in open space.
> 
> Live spike: https://outgrown.vercel.app
