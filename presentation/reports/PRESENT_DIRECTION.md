# Film Presentation Direction — OUTGROWN

This document serves as the high-level roadmap and creative vision for the Day 11 demo film, "THE FRAME THAT COULDN’T HOLD".

## Creative Strategy
Our objective is to sell the product concept (the epistemic boundary contraction) rather than a dry walk-through of application controls. 

- **Audience**: Product leaders, founders, site reliability engineers, and technical recruiters.
- **Narrative Philosophy**: The product truth is that explanations can survive but lose their completeness. We model the visualization as an active reasoning instrument.
- **Visual Aesthetic**: Low-frequency hums, high-contrast typography, and rigid frame movement.
